## Job corrections

A cancellation written by another process during a native observation or durable intent write could
previously allow the next batch to dispatch. The job engine now checks the durable cancellation marker
at the last safe boundary before executor dispatch. Deterministic regressions exercise both intervals
and require zero executor calls, zero cue progress and no residual in-flight marker.

The dependency reader previously counted a preset's requested reference and returned native address
as two graph nodes. Distinct native addresses could therefore exhaust its advertised 64-preset bound
after only 32 reads. The correction counts actual preset definitions while retaining alias/cycle
deduplication and the four-level embedded dependency limit.
